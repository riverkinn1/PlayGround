How to keep the farm

01

Tend by day

Click a plot to till it. Click again to sow your selected seed, then once more to water. Ripe crops glow — click to harvest.

02

Build before dusk

Choose a tower card, then click open grass. Towers fire on their own. Click a placed tower to upgrade it; right-click to sell.

03

Hold the night

Click enemies to fling stones. Press Space for Sunburst. Every night survived makes the next one stranger.
